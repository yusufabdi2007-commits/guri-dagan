Your Business App — Explained

A simple overview of what the app does and how it helps you every day.

What This App Is

This is your personal business assistant — built just for you, for your parenting coaching work. Everything you need to run your coaching business is in one place. No switching between 10 different apps. No forgetting what you were working on. Everything is here.

What It Helps You Do
1

Never run out of content ideas

The app uses AI to suggest video topics for you — based on what Somali parents care about most. You never have to sit there wondering "what do I post today?"

2

Plan your videos without stress

There is a calendar where you can drag and drop your content. You can see exactly what is coming up this week, next week, and beyond.

3

Track your progress

The app tracks your posting streak — how many days in a row you have shown up. It gives you a momentum score and encourages you when you are doing well.

4

Review your videos before posting

Before you post a video, you can open it inside the app and let the AI highlight the best and weakest moments — so you know exactly what to fix and what to keep.

5

Manage your coaching clients

You have a contact list for your clients — their sessions, follow-ups, and notes — all in one place.

6

Get weekly reports

Every week, the app gives you an AI-written summary: what is working, what is not, and what to focus on next week.

7

Let people book you

There is a public booking page where clients can request a session with you — it goes straight into your dashboard.

Who Built It and Why

Your son built this specifically for your business. It is not a generic app — every feature was designed around how you work, what you sell, and who your audience is: Somali parents.

It runs on your phone like a normal app. You can install it from the browser — no App Store needed. It is always there, always ready, built just for you.
