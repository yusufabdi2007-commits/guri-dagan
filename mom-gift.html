Understanding the App

What is this, and why does it matter?

Running a coaching business alone is hard. You have to come up with content ideas every single day. You have to remember which clients you need to follow up with. You have to track which videos are working. You have to plan your week, manage your bookings, and still show up for your family.

Most people juggle five or six different apps to do all of this — and still things fall through the cracks. Things get forgotten. Momentum gets lost.

This app puts everything in one place. It was built specifically around your work as a Somali parenting coach on YouTube. Every feature solves a real problem you face.

"Verily, with hardship comes ease."

Surah Al-Inshirah 94:6  ·  The Holy Quran

Allah promises ease after hardship. This app is part of that ease — so that the hard work you put into your mission is supported by smart tools, not held back by chaos.

The Features

Everything the app does for you

1

Never run out of content ideas

The app uses artificial intelligence to suggest video topics based on what Somali parents are searching for and struggling with. You will never sit down to record and think "what do I talk about today?" — the app already has ideas waiting for you.

2

Plan your week without stress

There is a calendar inside the app where you can plan which video goes out which day. You can drag and drop everything. You can see your whole month at a glance. No more mental load of trying to remember what is coming next.

3

Your progress is always visible

The app tracks your posting streak — how many days in a row you have shown up and posted content. It gives you a momentum score. On days when you feel tired or unmotivated, the app reminds you how far you have already come.

4

Review your videos with AI guidance

Before you post any video, you can open it inside the app. The AI will watch it and mark the best moments, the weakest moments, where viewers might stop watching, and where you connected emotionally. You make better videos, faster.

5

All your coaching clients in one place

The app has a client management section. Every client, their sessions, your notes, follow-up tasks — all stored safely, all searchable. You never forget a name or miss a follow-up again.

6

A weekly business report, written by AI

Every week, the app produces a full report: what content performed well, what did not, what your momentum score is, and what to focus on next week. It is like having a business advisor — for free, always available.

7

Clients can book you directly

There is a booking page anyone can visit — no login needed. A parent interested in your coaching fills in a simple form, and the request lands straight in your dashboard. No emails, no missed messages.
